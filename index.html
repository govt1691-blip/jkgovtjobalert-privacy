# Privacy Policy **App Name:** J&K Govt Job Alert **Last Updated:** 3 November 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to J&K Govt Job Alert. J&K Govt Job Alert is an independent informational mobile application designed to help users access information about government job vacancies, recruitment notifications, admit cards, examination dates, and results, particularly in Jammu and Kashmir. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our application. ## 2. Information We Collect The application may collect or process the following categories of information: - **Device information:** Device identifiers, operating system information, and technical details necessary for application functionality. - **Advertising information:** Google AdMob may collect advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, and other information to provide and measure advertisements. - **Notification information:** Firebase Cloud Messaging may process device registration tokens to deliver job alerts and other notifications. - **Diagnostic information:** Technical and performance information may be processed to maintain application stability and security. The application does not require users to submit personal details simply to browse publicly available job notifications. ## 3. How We Use Information Information may be used to: - Display government recruitment updates, admit cards, and examination results. - Send push notifications about newly published updates. - Display advertisements through Google AdMob. - Maintain application functionality, security, and performance. - Diagnose technical problems. ## 4.
