# Privacy Policy **App Name:** J&K Govt Job Alert **Last Updated:** 3 November 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to J&K Govt Job Alert. J&K Govt Job Alert is an independent informational mobile application that provides updates about government job vacancies, recruitment notifications, admit cards, examination dates, and results, particularly in Jammu and Kashmir. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is collected, used, stored, and shared through our application. ## 2. Information We Collect Our application may collect or process the following information through integrated services: - **Device information:** Device identifiers, operating system details, and technical information necessary for app functionality. - **Advertising information:** Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, advertisement interactions, and related data. - **Notification information:** Firebase Cloud Messaging may process device registration tokens to deliver notifications. - **Diagnostic information:** Technical and performance data may be processed to improve application stability, security, and performance. Users are not required to provide personal information simply to browse publicly available job notifications. ## 3. How We Use Information Information may be used to: - Display government job vacancies and recruitment updates. - Provide admit card, examination, and result information. - Send notifications about new recruitment announcements. - Display advertisements through Google AdMob. - Maintain application security and performance. - Identify and resolve technical issues. ## 4. Third-Party Services Our application uses third-party services that may process information according to their respective privacy policies. **Google Firebase** Firebase provides application infrastructure, cloud database functionality, and push notification services. **Google AdMob** Google AdMob displays advertisements and may process advertising identifiers and advertisement interaction information. For more information, please review: - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy - Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy - Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/ ## 5. Information Sharing We do not directly sell users' personal information. Certain device, advertising, notification, and technical information may be processed by Google and its service providers to deliver app functionality, notifications, analytics where enabled, and advertising. ## 6. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through our application, including using established service providers and appropriate security controls. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion Information processed through third-party services may be retained according to their applicable policies and technical requirements. Users may contact the developer using the email address below to request information about applicable data access or deletion options. Requests will be reviewed based on the information held by the developer and the capabilities of the relevant service providers. ## 8. Push Notifications J&K Govt Job Alert may send notifications about: - Government job vacancies. - Recruitment notifications. - Admit cards. - Examination dates. - Results and related updates. Users can manage or disable notifications through their device settings. ## 9. Children's Privacy The application is primarily intended for individuals seeking employment and recruitment information. It is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. ## 10.
